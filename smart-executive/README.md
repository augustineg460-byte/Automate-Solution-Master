# Smart Executive

**Automate Solutions – Powered by OpenAI**

**Tagline:** AI Powered. With Infinite Possibilities.

## Product Position

Smart Executive is an AI Executive Office for an individual. It is designed to make daily professional, business, and personal administration easier through voice, drafting, structured records, reminders, search, and automation.

## First Pilot

The first working pilot will be customised for the Owner/Promoter of Automate Solutions and tested with real Automate Solutions records.

## Core Modules

1. Daily Briefing
2. Personal Command Centre
3. AI Draft Studio
4. Voice Commands
5. Multilingual Speech-to-Text
6. Text-to-Speech and Voice Guidance
7. Camera and Document Input
8. Contacts and Address Records
9. Birthdays and People Reminders
10. Bills, Renewals, Insurance, and Financial Reminders
11. Appointments, Tasks, and Commitments
12. Google Sheet and Structured-Data Integration
13. Quick Search and Retrieval
14. Notifications and Follow-ups
15. Reports and Dashboard
16. Secure Login and Data Export

## Important Rules

- Keep every client’s information private and separate.
- Do not mix materials from different products or clients.
- Voice support must not remove normal typing.
- The interface must be simple for non-technical users.
- Fonts and controls must be clearly readable.
- Test all important functions on mobile and laptop.
- A demo must be clearly distinguished from a production-ready working version.
